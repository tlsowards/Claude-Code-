## Shorts (vertical, under 60 seconds each)

### Short 1: Rogers v. City of Santa Fe

- Hook (on screen and spoken): A use of force review board that reads this outcome as validation of the technique has misread a legal document.
- Body: Teach the scan. When you put a person prone and intend to hold them there, look at what you are holding them on and move them if you can; moving a controlled subject three feet takes about two seconds and is almost always possible. Train it as part of the technique, not as a separate judgment call, because under adrenaline officers will not make a separate judgment call.
- Card: Rogers v. City of Santa Fe, Fifth Circuit, August 6, 2026 (opinion filed); publicly surfaced September 9, 2026

### Short 2: Mitchell v. Conrad

- Hook (on screen and spoken): There are two immunity systems running in parallel, the federal one and whatever the state provides, and they are not the same test.
- Body: Camera position is the difference between summary judgment and a trial; the recording showed the vehicle moving and officers in its path. Be honest with officers about why the animus affidavits failed: they were general, and said something about the man rather than about the moment. An agency with an officer who has a documented pattern should not take comfort in this holding, because the next plaintiffs will build the record moment specific, and the early intervention system is the document they will build it out of.
- Card: Mitchell v. Conrad, Sixth Circuit, May 14, 2026

A qualified immunity win is not a finding that the force was reasonable.

Tell command staff and the review board in plain words. Since Pearson, courts may skip the constitutional question and decide only whether a prior case was close enough, and most do. All three cases this week were won that way. Novelty is a one-time asset that burns off: once an opinion is published and cited, the conduct it describes becomes clearly established for everyone who comes after, so training to the immunity line is training to a line that moves against you every year.
