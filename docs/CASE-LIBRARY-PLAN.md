## Source notes

- Supreme Court opinions: Library of Congress U.S. Reports PDFs and supremecourt.gov slip opinions.
- Federal Reporter and Ninth Circuit opinions: Public.Resource.Org archive and the Ninth Circuit's own PDFs.
- California appellate opinions and federal district court opinions (Sager, Doe v. Borough of Barrington, Popow, Pham): no public source was reachable from the build environment in November 2026. These briefs wait on a source (Dr. Sowards' own copies, or a CourtListener account).
- Three Ninth Circuit opinions were also unreachable because the court's archive has gaps (2007 to 2008 files and some later ones): Gregory v. County of Maui, Andrews v. City of Henderson, and O'Doan v. Sanford. They wait on the same source.
- Docket and date corrections found while reading: Tatum v. City and County of San Francisco is No. 04-15055 (course materials say 03-16286); Hart v. City of Grand Rapids is Sixth Circuit No. 23-1382; Chinaryan was filed August 14, 2024; Villanueva was filed January 28, 2021; the Ninth Circuit's Scott v. Smith opinion was filed July 30, 2024 and vacated April 20, 2026.
- Items the course materials already flag as unconfirmed: Pfau v. Kim's Hapkido (no published opinion found), Richardson v. U.S. (docket only), Levine v. City of New York (a settlement, not an opinion), Fronk v. Meager and Bell v. Crow (citations not located).
