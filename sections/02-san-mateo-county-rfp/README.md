## Scoring context

Phase 1, 100 points: Project Approach 30, Trainer Qualifications and Experience 35,
Safety and Compliance 10, Innovation 5, Cost 15, Preferential Points 5.

Sowards Consulting is Sacramento based and forfeits the 5 local preference points, so a
San Mateo County competitor starts 5 points ahead. The bid has to win on the 35-point
qualifications block and the 30-point approach block.

Phase 2 interview, 60 minutes: Introductions 5, Vendor Presentation 20, Standardized
Questions 40, **Scenario-Based Exercise 20**, Vendor Questions 10, Closing 5.

## Judgment calls already made, do not reverse

1. **Travel is bundled into the rates but still documented.** Section 4.3.1.F asks for
   travel identified via GSA per diem. Writing only "travel included" leaves F unanswered.
   The travel worksheet documents the absorbed GSA-based cost, which answers F and
   strengthens the cost criterion.
2. **Category J is framed around understanding the accountability process**, not labor
   advocacy. The audience is a Professional Standards Unit, often the internal affairs
   function. Union experience is presented as insight into why officers fail ethically,
   not as representation experience.
3. **Cost per student is computed at maximum fill**, with a session-minimum note, because
   the firm prices by session while the County's table demands per-student figures.
4. **Narrow category bid over broad.** Four categories with credential backing beats ten
   thin ones.
5. **Current county employment appears on the CV.** It is a credibility asset but makes
   outside employment visible to another agency's committee. Tim decides.
