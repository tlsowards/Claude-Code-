This form keeps a working draft in this browser only — nothing is transmitted anywhere, and no data reaches CAFOP until you print or export it. The draft persists, including your name and email, until you use Clear form. On a shared or public computer, clear it when you are done.

Rate basis: meals follow the GSA FY2026 standard CONUS M&IE rate ($68/day, effective 1 Oct 2025 – 30 Sep 2026); mileage follows the IRS 2026 standard business rates (72.5¢/mi 1 Jan – 30 Jun 2026; 76¢/mi from 1 Jul 2026). Lodging is reimbursed at actual cost with no nightly cap. Because the meal per diem is paid at the federal rate and lodging is receipted at actual cost, the whole claim sits inside an accountable plan — none of it is reportable as income to the member. Neither agency varies the mileage rate by trip distance, and neither does this form. Capping mileage at the cost of a comparable flight is CAFOP policy, modeled on the federal constructive-cost rule. Update this form when FY2027 rates publish in late August 2026.
