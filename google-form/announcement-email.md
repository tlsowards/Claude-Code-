# Announcement email to officers

Send this once the Form is set up and you have tested it. Replace `PASTE THE
PUBLISHED FORM URL HERE` with the published URL that `setUp` printed in the
execution log, and sign it with your own name and office.

Keep it short — this is a two-minute read that should make the form feel easier
than what it replaces, not like a new procedure to learn.

---

**Subject:** New reimbursement form — one link, no math

Officers,

Travel reimbursement claims now go through an online form. One link, works on your
phone, no login required:

**PASTE THE PUBLISHED FORM URL HERE**

**What changed for you:** nothing you have to learn. The form asks what you paid
and how far you drove. It works out the per diem, the mileage rate and the totals
itself, and emails you a copy of the finished claim within a minute so you can
check it before I do.

You no longer need to know the meal rate, look up the mileage rate, or add
anything up.

**Before you start, have these handy:**

- Your travel dates — departure and return. These drive the meal per diem, so get
  them right.
- The hotel folio: nights, room rate before tax, and taxes and fees.
- Round-trip miles driven and the date you drove. The mileage rate changed on
  1 July, so the date matters.
- How many breakfasts, lunches or dinners somebody else fed you — conference
  meals, hosted dinners. Those get deducted.
- Anything else you paid for, with receipts. Registration, parking, baggage,
  rideshare.
- Whether you already got an advance or put any of it on an association card.

**A few things worth knowing:**

- **Meals need no receipts.** They are paid at the federal per diem rate. Every
  other expense does need one.
- **Lodging is reimbursed at what you actually paid.** There is no nightly cap.
  Attach the itemized folio showing a zero balance.
- **If you drove more than 200 miles round trip,** include a screenshot of what a
  comparable coach flight would have cost, taken when you booked. Association
  policy reimburses the lesser of the two. You are always free to drive — this
  only affects the amount.
- **File within 30 days of getting back.**

Submit it and you will get an emailed copy with the total. If a number looks wrong,
reply to that email and we will fix it before payment goes out.

Questions, ask me.

YOUR NAME
YOUR OFFICE, CAFOP
