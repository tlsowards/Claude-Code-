## What this package is

A review of the department's PREA posture at the Youth Detention Facility, and the documents produced from it. Everything here traces to one worksheet, the gap register, which assesses 91 requirements from 28 CFR Part 115 Subpart D and California Title 15 against the 17 departmental documents produced to the review.

- **The analysis is complete.** Nothing in this package is waiting on further review.

- **Most of the remediation is drafted.** Amended policy text exists for seven of the fifteen recommended changes, written out and ready to be marked up.

- **What is left needs decisions, not analysis.** A designation, an assignment, and seven documents the review asked for and has not received. Those are listed at the end.

- **Nothing here is a finding that the department failed to do something.** The review can only speak to the documents produced to it. Where a practice is not written down, the finding says it does not appear in the documents reviewed, which is a different statement. Several findings will close on production of a document rather than on any change in practice.

### Attachment H. OO 1453 with the fixes marked

### Attachment J. OO 1352.5, citations and required actions

## How to use this package

Nobody needs to read all of it. By role:

- **Administrators and managers: Attachment A, and page 3 of it is the whole review in five numbered points.** The rest of A is the backup for each point, one page apiece. Twenty minutes.

- **Whoever owns policy revision: Attachment B**, the amended text for the first seven changes, then **Attachment C** for the full requirement by requirement record.

- **Supervisors: Attachment E**, one sheet printed double sided. Attachment D is the longer version behind it, read once in advance rather than during an incident, and Attachment F is the memo written after an incident is classified.

- **Whoever answers the staffing question: Attachment G.**
