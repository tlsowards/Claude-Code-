# PREA Incident Classification Card

**One page. Five steps. Work them in order.**

> DRAFT FOR COUNTY COUNSEL REVIEW. Not for issuance to supervisors until the CANRA column is confirmed.

Ages first. Age decides the CPS answer before you even look at what happened. Everything else, who investigates and where you stop, is in the Decision Guide.

---

## Step 1. Ages first

Settle this first. Anyone 18 or over is not a child for CANRA, whatever the court says.

| Who is involved | What happens to the CPS column |
|---|---|
| Both under 18 | Use the chart as written. |
| One under 18, one over | Use the chart as written. **Never ask whether they consented.** A minor cannot consent with an adult. |
| Both 18 or over | **No CPS report, any tier.** No child, no CANRA. PREA still applies. Sexual assault goes to the **Sheriff**. |

## Step 2. What happened

Two separate questions. They match on touching and part company everywhere else.

| Tier | What happened | Is it PREA? | CPS report? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | One sexual comment. No contact, no exposure, no threat. | Not PREA | **No** |
| **2** | Sexual comments or gestures, repeated, same youth. | Sexual harassment | **No** |
| **3** | Exposes genitals. Nothing else. | Harassment if repeated | **No. Read tier 4** |
| **4** | Masturbates where another youth can see. | Harassment if repeated | **YES if the youth who saw it is under 18** |
| **5** | Touches genitals, anus, groin, breast, inner thigh, buttocks. Clothed counts. | SEXUAL ABUSE | **YES if either is under 18** |
| **6** | Oral, anal or penetrative contact. Or anything above by force. | SEXUAL ABUSE | **YES if either is under 18** |

## Step 3. Check for a flip

Any one of these moves it up a tier and forces a CPS call.

- Force, threat, or leverage of any kind. Debt, protection, commissary.
- Any sign it happened before.
- The target is under 14.
- A big age, size, or ability gap.
- The target mentions past abuse, here or outside.
- You suspect the youth who acted was abused too. **That is a second report, about a different child.**
- Same youth, more than one target.
- **Any staff involvement.** Off this chart. Always reports.

## Step 4. Act

Each row adds to the one above it.

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Every tier** | Separate them. Check the target is safe. Tell the Duty Supervisor. Write an incident report naming both youth, **with the words they used**. Record your tier and why, even if you decide it is nothing. |
| **Tier 2 and up** | Log it as a PREA incident. If the target is at risk right now, protect them now, before anyone investigates. Reassess housing for both. Start retaliation checks if anyone reported. |
| **Tier 4 and up, where CPS is due** | **Phone it in now.** Form SS 8572 within 36 hours. Do not wait for the investigation, the end of shift, or permission. It belongs to whoever formed the suspicion. Telling you does not discharge it. |
| **Tiers 5 and 6** | Treat it as abuse from the first minute. **Keep the evidence.** No showering, no cleaning up. Refer it. Every allegation gets investigated. Tier 6 also means law enforcement, a forensic exam off site, and notice to parent or guardian and counsel. |

## Step 5. Write it down

Every time. Even when you decide it is nothing.

```
Tier: ____    Conduct in one line: ______________________________
Ages: both under 18 / one under one over / both 18 or over
PREA call: Not PREA  /  Sexual harassment  /  SEXUAL ABUSE
Flip factors present: none / ____________________________________
CPS report: made at ______ by ______  /  not made, because ______
Protection and housing action taken: ____________________________
Retaliation monitoring started: yes / no / not applicable
Supervisor: ______________________  Date and time: ______________
```
